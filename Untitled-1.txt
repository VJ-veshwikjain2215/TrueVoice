## How TrueVoice Operates
TrueVoice was engineered to address generative audio impersonation. Neural voice generation can clone a human voice in seconds, but leaves distinct acoustic anomalies in the frequency, vocoder phase, and pitch stability domains.

### Phase Incoherence in High Frequencies
Neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, MelGAN) invert mel-spectrograms to generate speech waveforms. Because phase data is estimated, micro-phase discontinuities appear in the 4 kHz - 8 kHz bands that do not exist in organic vocal cords.

### Biometric Voiceprint Matching
TrueVoice computes high-dimensional acoustic embeddings (Resemblyzer GE2E architecture) to evaluate cosine similarity against an enrolled speaker. A voice must match the contact identity (≥ 0.75) and pass anti-spoofing to be declared authentic.

### Median-of-Five Session Filtering
To prevent momentary acoustic anomalies or background coughs from triggering false alerts, TrueVoice enforces a 5-window sliding buffer that requires 2 consecutive elevated windows to escalate and 4 to de-escalate.
